# Stand8
> Consulting and staffing partner that channels client engagements to Modern Stack Systems.

## Overview
Stand8 serves as an intermediary between Modern Stack Systems and end clients, managing multiple concurrent engagements across different industries. The partnership allows MSS to scale service delivery through Stand8's client relationships and sales channels. Stand8's Solutions Engineering team actively supports project delivery and client coordination.
