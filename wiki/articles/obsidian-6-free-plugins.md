# These 6 FREE Obsidian Plugins Quietly Replace $200 of Paid Apps

> Six free plugins turn Obsidian into a productivity OS, replacing Notion, Todoist, Trello, Fantastical, TextExpander, and backup tools.

**Source:** [6 Free Obsidian Plugins (@kanikabk)](https://x.com/kanikabk/status/2042646783852229099?s=46) | **Published:** 2026-04-10 | **Tags:** tech, learning

## Summary
A rundown of six free Obsidian plugins that consolidate a stack of paid subscriptions into one vault: Dataview (queryable databases over frontmatter/tags, replaces Notion), Tasks (in-note to-dos with due dates/recurrence/filters, replaces Todoist), Kanban (visual boards, replaces Trello), Calendar (date navigation for daily notes, replaces Fantastical), Templater (reusable templates with variables/logic, replaces TextExpander), and Obsidian Git (versioned vault backup/restore). The real win framed as one connected system rather than fragmented apps.

## Key Takeaways
- Starter stack: Tasks, Dataview, Templater, Calendar, Obsidian Git, Kanban.
- Dataview = structure, Templater + Tasks = speed, Git = reliability.
- Several of these (Dataview, Templater, Git) are already part of the Second Brain architecture here.

## Why It Matters
Directly relevant to maintaining this vault. Dataview powers the dashboards; Templater backs quick-capture; Git gives version history for the wiki.
